03 — Background

“I'm a partnerships operator, not just a strategist — embedded enough to move deals forward, senior enough to set the strategy.”

I'm Brandi, founder of DAC Sports & Media. My work sits at the intersection of brand activation and events, sponsorship agreements and negotiation, brand campaign planning, and strategic partnerships — built on a foundation of brand strategy, audience insight, and vendor and talent management.

Over the course of my career I've overseen $50M+ in distribution and marketing commitments, generated $20M+ in revenue across more than 100 paid media partnership campaigns, and built and scaled a $10M+ sponsorship portfolio spanning live events and digital platforms. I currently manage 25+ active content partner relationships, including Disney, Warner Bros., NBCUniversal, and Amazon, and I've led sponsorship programs for 45+ teams across the Big 5 sports leagues.

I've also worked directly with Olympic and Paralympic organizations — including US Swimming, US Ski & Snowboarding, and US Track & Field — managing partnerships that require precision, trust, and long-term relationship stewardship. Across 150+ end-to-end brand partnerships, my campaigns have driven an average subscription lift of 10–13%.

Today, I bring that same operating experience directly to brands, platforms, and talent — from first concept to signed agreement.
