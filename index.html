The Opportunity

Women's sports is one of the fastest-growing categories in sports business, and most brands and athletes are still figuring out how to show up.

Women's sports sponsorship grew 17.5% in 2025, more than 3.5x the rate of men's leagues.

Source: SponsorUnited, 2026 Women's Sports Sponsorship Report

Brands need to know which athletes to partner with, how to activate so fans care, and how to prove it worked. Athletes need to know what brands look for, what they're worth, and how to turn one deal into many. That's where we come in.
