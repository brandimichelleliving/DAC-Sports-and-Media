I'm Brandi, founder of DAC Sports & Media. After years working across brand partnerships, sponsorships, and media distribution — including sponsorship programs for 45+ teams across the Big 5 sports leagues and partnerships with Olympic and Paralympic organizations including US Swimming, US Ski & Snowboarding, and US Track & Field — I kept running into the same problem: brands wanted to invest in women's sports, but didn't know how to do it well.

Over my career I've overseen $50M+ in distribution and marketing commitments, managed 25+ active content partner relationships with organizations including Disney, Warner Bros., NBCUniversal, and Amazon, and led more than 150 end-to-end brand partnerships. That's operating experience, not just strategy — the actual work of selecting partners, negotiating agreements, and running activations. That's what DAC now brings specifically to brands entering women's sports.

Today, I help brands find the right athlete partners, build activations that resonate with women's sports audiences, and measure whether it worked — while also supporting athletes directly through free profile audits, with an eye toward formal representation down the line.

“Women's sports doesn't need a checkbox. It needs a real strategy — that's what I build.”
